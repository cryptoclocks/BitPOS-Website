Built during the hackathon

Added between 26 October and 4 November 2026, on top of the existing BitPOS point of sale.

TAP

Messenger opens at your table

One consent button and no sign-up. NTAG 424 one-time links are checked on the server.

OFFER

Muse Spark picks, the server prices

The model chooses from offers the owner approved. It never sets a price.

CHAT

Order by text or voice

Muse Spark edits a real cart with server tools, in Thai or English. Stripe Checkout takes the payment.

ALLOWANCE

Let the AI pay, within limits

From one day to three months, with a cap per order and in total. Stop at any time.

BOOK

Food waits until you tap in

Book in chat from free times only. Pre-orders stay out of the kitchen queue until check-in.

CONNECTOR

Your own agent can order

An MCP connector gives agents the menu, a server quote and checkout. They pass IDs only.

OWNER

AI drafts, a person approves

Muse Spark drafts promotions and Muse Image makes banners. Nothing goes live without approval.

EVIDENCE

Every model call on record

The owner's ledger shows each AI call, tap, Stripe event and promotion decision.

ONE SHOP

One POS for every channel

Messenger, table and agent orders meet in one place, with offers on the BitPOS Mini.

Where it stands today

  • Deployed and running with the Muse Café demo shop and sample menu data.
  • Stripe sandbox only, using a US test merchant. No real money moves.
  • The Meta app is in development mode, so Messenger only answers accounts with a role on the app.
  • The physical card on Table 1 uses a plain link that can be copied. The NTAG 424 one-time check is built and tested with simulated taps.
  • Inside the Muse app, each purchase still asks for approval. The AI allowance applies to our Messenger chat and connector.
  • The BitPOS point of sale and BitPOS Mini existed before the hackathon. The provenance report separates old and new work.
